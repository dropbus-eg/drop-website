ABOUT DROP

Built around the school journey.

DROP is a software platform that helps schools organize transport and makes journey information clearer for bus teams and parents.

01

When journey information is scattered

Route planning, student check-ins and parent updates need to work together. DROP brings them into one experience so each person can see what matters to them.

02

Focused on school days

We design around the real rhythm of school transport: preparing routes and students, following a trip and sharing arrival updates.

What guides the work

01

Clarity

Information that is easy to understand when a team or parent needs it.

02

Coordination

A shared way to connect schools, bus teams and families.

03

Thoughtful design

Practical tools shaped around daily school transport work.
