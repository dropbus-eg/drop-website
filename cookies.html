Cookie Policy

How DROP uses cookies and similar technologies across our website and app

Last Updated: March 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit our website. They help the site remember your preferences, keep you signed in, and let us understand how the site is used so we can improve it.

2. Cookies We Use

TypePurposeDuration
EssentialRequired for the site to function — e.g. remembering your cookie consent choice and keeping pages secureSession / 1 year
PreferencesRemember settings such as language and display preferences1 year
AnalyticsHelp us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it (anonymized where possible)Up to 2 years

3. Managing Cookies

  • When you first visit our site, you can choose to accept or decline non-essential cookies via the banner
  • You can change or withdraw your consent at any time by clearing your browser's cookies for this site and reloading the page
  • Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings — note that disabling essential cookies may affect how the site works

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some pages may load content from third parties (such as map or font providers) that can set their own cookies. We do not control these cookies — please refer to the relevant third party's policy for details.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last Updated" date.

6. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or how we use cookies? Email us at dropbus.eg@gmail.com — we respond within 5 business days. See also our Privacy Policy.
