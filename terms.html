2. Service Description

Drop provides reserved seat booking on daily shared air-conditioned buses between Alexandria and Smart Village, Cairo. The service includes a mobile booking app, live GPS tracking, and a guaranteed named seat.

3. Booking Terms

  • Users must be 16 years or older to complete a booking
  • Bookings are personal and non-transferable
  • A booking is confirmed upon payment completion and confirmation message receipt
  • Passengers must arrive at the pickup point at least 5 minutes before departure
