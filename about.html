Our Story

We built DROP because commuting was broken

Every day, millions of Egyptians waste hours in traffic. We set out to fix that — one reliable, air-conditioned ride at a time.

Why We Exist
The problem we set out to solve
Six months ago, our founders in Sporting, Alexandria spent hours commuting to Smart Village every day. The options were unreliable microbuses, expensive taxis with surge pricing, or uncomfortable ride-hails.

They built DROP to give Egypt's professional workforce something it deserved: a reliable, affordable, dignified way to commute. Fixed prices. Guaranteed seats. On time — every time.

Today, DROP is officially live on five routes — Alexandria–Smart Village, Tagamo3, 6th October, Maadi, and Nasr City — and we're just getting started.

What Drives Us
Our core values

Safety First, Always

Every decision we make starts with safety. From driver vetting to real-time tracking, we never compromise on the security of our riders and drivers.

Radical Transparency

No hidden fees. No surge pricing. No surprises. We believe you deserve to know exactly what you're paying before you confirm any ride.

Reliability Above All

We understand that being late can cost you an opportunity. Our systems are built to ensure drivers show up — and on time — no exceptions.

Community Impact

We're not just a commute app — we're creating employment for thousands of Egyptian drivers while reducing congestion and carbon emissions.

Constant Innovation

From AI-powered route optimization to smart scheduling — we continuously invest in technology to make every ride better than the last.

Dignity for All

Whether you're a rider or a driver, you deserve respect and dignity. DROP is built on mutual trust, fairness, and human connection.

Our Journey
From idea to impact
6 Months Ago

The Idea is Born

Co-founders Abdelrahman and Omar, both exhausted by their daily Alexandria → Smart Village commute, decide to build a better way. DROP is born in Sporting, Alexandria.

4 Months Ago

Developing & Building

The core team focuses entirely on building the tech stack from scratch, setting up rigorous operations, and securing top-tier vehicles.

2 Months Ago

Beta Testing Phase

We ran exclusive beta tests where early users tried out the service. Their feedback helped us refine the routes, perfect the app experience, and ensure 100% reliability.

Official Launch

DROP is officially live! We are launching our platform to the public, ready to transform the daily commute for professionals across Egypt.
