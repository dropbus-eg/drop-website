How do I book a ride on DROP?
Simply download the app, enter your destination, choose your preferred vehicle type, and confirm. The whole process takes under 10 seconds. Review your trip details before confirming.
How are drivers verified?
Every DROP driver goes through a multi-step verification process including criminal background checks, vehicle inspection, and customer service training. Drivers are continuously monitored via ratings and performance reviews.
Can I schedule a ride in advance?
Yes! With the Weekly Bundle, you can schedule rides up to 14 days in advance. Perfect for early morning commutes, airport runs, or important meetings where you can't be late.
How does the corporate plan work?
Our corporate plan gives you a company dashboard to manage all employee rides, set spending limits, access real-time tracking, and receive a consolidated monthly invoice. Perfect for teams of 5 or more. Contact our sales team for a custom quote.
Which cities does DROP currently serve?
DROP currently runs daily between Alexandria and five destinations: Smart Village, Tagamo3 (New Cairo), 6th October, Maadi, and Nasr City. We're expanding every month — follow our social media for new route announcements.
